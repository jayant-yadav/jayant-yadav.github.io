## My return to biking
The 2026 swedish winter had some very cold days and nights. Added to the fact that I was visiting my girlfriend in London, made me switch to public transport in order to conserve some energy and stay warm. It was not until mid April that I returned back to biking, which was roughly around 4 month long break. 


## Bike repair
Before I could hop onto my bike and once again enjoy the onset of summer, I found my bike in a miserable state. Even though it was bought in the summer of 2025, lack of care and harsh winter had caused some major rusting issues in its chain and around gears. When I shamefully opened my garage and unlocked my bike, I felt like it was giving me a side eye as if I had betrayed it. A gently touched its seat and appologized. I had to. It was my fault afterall, that I did not even pay attention to it. 

I laid down the bike on the unfrozen grass in my garden and began inspecting its parts. Rust everywhere. The chain was not budging at all and the front gear was frozen on high. I started by applying some antigrease, rubbed it in some parts of the chain, left it for a couple of minutes and then brushed it with a kitchen cloth. The rust barely cleared off. I knew it needed some strong antirust chemical, so I sparyed some to test its affect on one part of the chain and then brushed it off. I repeated this a few times until I could move some elements in the chain. Clearly I replace the chain as cleaning off the rust seamed futile. 

Luckily I found a replacement chain at Biltema and with it, a dechaining tool. I was so eager to replace my chain that evening, that I came home and started the process as the sun was setting. Within a few minutes, it darker and I had to work under my phone's light. While trying to break of the chain, I accidently placed the dechainer tool incorrectly, which caused it to bend, than to pull out the pin from the link. After correcting the tool positioning, I was able to unpin one link and break the chain off! Then I carefully wrapped the new chain, hoping that its the right length and right wrap around the front chainring and rear cassette. Fearing alignment issues, when I finally clicked in the last link of the chain and peddled it backwards, I finally took a sigh of relief. I was also proud of myself that I did replaced the chain and repaired my bike myself.  


## The biking experience
I was overconfident that I could, just like any other day, bike to work without feeling out of breath. Afterall, I have been biking around Uppsala this past 4 years. As I sat on my bike after 4 months, the first thing I noticed was that the seat is not as comfortable as I tought it would be (I still have some soarness from sitting on it after almost 2 days). But as I started biking again, it felt liberating. I felt like after all this while I had constrained myself to a rhythm that was finally breaking. Felt like I have regained my freedom. I could go to the city center if I wished to, or go to visit a friend. It's not like I had less options with public transport or even if I possessed a car. It's just that picking up my bike and reaching anywhere felt more feasiable. Ofcourse I am limiting my choice radius to a few kms. There is less friction while taking a decision as I have no more have to look at bus timetables. 

My breathing improves when I bike too. Quick inhales and longer exhales help me warm up and prepare my legs for all that work. Then I settle to a steady breathing pace. It is almost mechanical while biking a flat surface, as my mind go on autopilot mode. This gives space for me to indulge in my thoughts and ideas if I wish to. It is quite meditative at times.
